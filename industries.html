Industries

Different sectors, different pressures.

A bank, a factory and a school all need reliable, secure IT, but the details differ a great deal. We adapt our designs, documentation and way of working to the realities of each sector.

Talk to an engineer

Government & Semi-Government

Public entities must protect citizen data and keep services available. We deliver infrastructure, security controls and documentation aligned with NCA requirements, and we are comfortable with formal procurement, strict change control and detailed reporting.

NCA ECC alignmentSecure emailChange control

Banking & Financial Services

Institutions and fintechs operate under close supervision. We support them with gap assessments against the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, access and data protection controls, security testing and the evidence auditors ask for.

SAMA CSF gap assessmentSecurity testingAudit evidence

Industrial & Manufacturing

Plants, yards and warehouses need networks and cameras that keep working in heat, dust and over long distances. We provide industrial-grade networking, fiber backbones, wide-area surveillance and separation between plant and office systems.

Fiber backbonesWide-area CCTVOT separation

Healthcare

Hospitals and clinics cannot afford downtime, and patient records must stay private. We provide resilient networks, dependable backup, secure access for clinical staff and maintenance scheduled around patient care.

Resilient networksBackup & recoverySecure access

Education

Schools, institutes and universities need safe campus-wide Wi-Fi, content filtering, learning platforms that stay online and campus security systems, all within tight budgets.

Campus Wi-FiContent filteringCampus security

Retail & Hospitality

Shops, restaurants and hotels depend on point-of-sale, guest Wi-Fi, cameras and online ordering working together at every branch. We standardize the setup so each new location opens quickly.

Multi-branch networksGuest Wi-FiPOS & CCTV

Real Estate & Facilities

Developers and facility managers need building-wide cabling, access control, surveillance, intercoms and tenant connectivity designed before the walls are closed.

Building cablingAccess controlTenant connectivity

SMEs & Startups

Growing businesses need professional IT without a full IT department. We act as that department: setup, security, support and sensible advice that grows with the company.

IT setupManaged supportAffordable security
