Our story

Why we started Aureon

The idea came from the other side of the table. For more than two decades our leadership managed IT inside companies in the Kingdom, across industry, trading, contracting and services. We worked with many suppliers. Some were excellent. Too many were excellent only until the invoice was paid.

The pattern repeated: systems installed without documentation, security products sold with no one to configure them, cabling left unlabeled, projects declared finished while the users were still waiting for training.

We started Aureon to be the supplier we always wanted to hire: one team that can handle the whole job, engineers who explain what they are doing, complete paperwork on handover day, and support that continues long after go-live.

Our mission

To give organizations in Saudi Arabia technology they can depend on, by designing it carefully, installing it properly, securing it thoroughly and supporting it honestly.

Our vision

To be the IT partner Saudi businesses recommend to each other, known for work done right the first time and for being there when it matters.

What a new company means for you

Every project receives senior attention, from the first site visit to the final handover. Decisions are made quickly, without layers of approval. And every project we deliver now shapes the reputation we are building for the years ahead.

We would rather grow steadily with clients who trust us than quickly with work we cannot do properly.

Who does the work

Aureon is a company of experienced engineers. Network and security specialists, software developers, AI practitioners, cabling technicians and project managers work side by side, and the person who designs your system usually helps install it.

We hire for curiosity and care as much as for qualifications, and give every engineer time and budget to keep training.

Vision 2030

Building for the Kingdom’s next chapter

Vision 2030 asks every organization to work more digitally, more efficiently and more securely. Large enterprises have teams to manage that change. Many mid-sized and smaller businesses do not.

That is where we focus much of our effort. We bring the design, security and documentation standard large enterprises expect to companies still building their IT function, and help them meet national requirements such as the NCA cybersecurity controls, the Personal Data Protection Law and ZATCA e-invoicing without unnecessary cost.

Responsible technology

Good IT design and responsible use of resources go together. We size equipment for the real workload so clients are not powering and cooling hardware they do not need, recommend energy-efficient devices, consolidate servers through virtualization where it makes sense, and replace paper processes with digital ones.

When old equipment is retired, we wipe the data securely and arrange proper recycling or disposal.
