Managed Services

Your IT, looked after every day.

Most IT problems warn you first. A disk fills up. A backup fails quietly. A firewall license is about to expire. Someone has to be watching.

With a managed service from Aureon, we are. We monitor, maintain and support your systems under an agreed service level for a predictable monthly fee, so your people can focus on the business.

What we deliver

What we manage

Helpdesk & User Support

One point of contact for your staff by phone, email, WhatsApp or ticket portal. Everyday issues are solved remotely where possible, with an engineer on site where not. Arabic and English support.

Network Management

Monitoring of switches, routers, firewalls, Wi-Fi and internet links, fault response, firmware updates and configuration backups, for a network that performs consistently across offices and branches.

Security Monitoring & Management

We manage firewalls, endpoint protection, email security and access controls, review alerts and logs, and investigate and escalate suspicious activity under an agreed procedure.

Cloud, Server & Backup Management

Microsoft 365, cloud workloads and on-premises servers: user administration, patching, capacity, licensing and cost control. Backups are checked daily and test-restored on a schedule.

CCTV & Security System Maintenance

Cameras often fail quietly. Under contract we check camera health and recording, clean and realign cameras, update firmware and replace faulty units.

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled visits to inspect server rooms, UPS, cabling, network equipment and end-user devices, each ending with a short written report and recommended actions.

SLAs & Reporting

Every service has a written SLA with response and resolution targets per priority. A monthly report shows our performance against it, and we review it with you regularly.
