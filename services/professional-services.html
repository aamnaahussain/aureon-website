What we deliver

What we offer

IT Consulting & Strategy

We help you decide before you spend: business goals, current systems and budget become a practical roadmap of what to do now, what can wait and what it costs. Typical work: IT strategy and budgeting, technology selection, cloud readiness, preparing IT for growth or audit.

Assessments & Audits

An honest picture of where you stand across network, servers, security controls, backups, licenses and processes, reported in plain language with each finding’s risk, fix and effort. Many clients use the report to plan next year’s budget.

Solution Architecture & Integration

Requirements become detailed designs: network layouts, server and storage sizing, security architecture and integration points, reviewed with you before anything is ordered. Because we cover every layer, nothing falls between suppliers.

Pre-Staging & Rollout

Equipment is configured and tested on our bench before it reaches your site. Installation is scheduled around your working hours with a rollback plan for every change, and engineers stay close after go-live.

Project Management

One project manager is responsible for scope, schedule, budget and communication. You get short written updates at an agreed interval and risks are raised early, using recognized practice and ITIL-aligned change control.

Training & Knowledge Transfer

Practical sessions built around daily tasks, in Arabic or English. Handover includes as-built drawings, configuration records, credentials and operating procedures.

License & Warranty Tracking

A register of software licenses, support contracts and hardware warranties, with reminders well ahead of each renewal, so you avoid lapses and stop paying for what you no longer use.

Compliance

Designed with regulation in mind

Regulatory requirements in the Kingdom are detailed and actively enforced. We design and document each project with the frameworks that apply to you in view:

NCA ECC

Essential Cybersecurity Controls: baseline security across systems and operations.

SAMA CSF

Cyber Security Framework for banks, finance companies, insurers and fintechs.

PDPL & SDAIA

Personal Data Protection Law and SDAIA guidance on handling personal data.

ISO 27001 · ITIL

Recognized good practice for security and service management.

We design to these frameworks. We do not claim certification unless it is shown on this site.
