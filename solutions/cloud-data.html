FAQ

Common questions

Is the cloud secure?

A well-configured cloud is often more secure than a typical server room. Most cloud incidents come from configuration mistakes, so we apply security settings from day one and review them regularly.

Can we keep our data inside Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Several major providers operate or are opening regions inside the Kingdom, and local hosting exists as well. We help match each type of data to hosting that meets your obligations.

Will the cloud save us money?

Sometimes, not always. Savings usually come from retiring old hardware and paying only for what you use. You get a like-for-like comparison before you decide.

Does Microsoft 365 need its own backup?

We recommend it. Microsoft keeps the service running, but protecting data against accidental deletion, malicious deletion and ransomware is your responsibility. An independent backup closes that gap.
