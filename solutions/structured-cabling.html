Structured Cabling

The part of your network you install once and rely on for fifteen years.

Cabling is the least visible part of your IT and the hardest to change later. Done well, it carries every call, camera, access point and computer without complaint for a decade or more. Done badly, it causes faults nobody can trace.

We install to recognized standards, label every point, test every link and hand you the results.

What we deliver

Cabling services

Copper Cabling

Cat6 and Cat6A for data, voice, Wi-Fi, CCTV and access control, including Power over Ethernet to devices.

Fiber Optic Cabling

Single-mode and multi-mode fiber for building backbones and links between buildings, with fusion splicing and OTDR testing.

Racks, Cabinets & Patching

Racks, cabinets, patch panels, cable management and power distribution, laid out for airflow and access.

Containment & Pathways

Trunking, cable tray, conduit and floor boxes to suit the building, coordinated with other contractors on site.

Testing & Certification

Every copper and fiber link is tested with calibrated certification equipment. You receive the full report for every point.

Labeling & Documentation

A consistent labeling scheme at both ends of every cable, with as-built drawings and a patching schedule.

Re-cabling & Clean-up

We untangle and reorganize existing cabinets and server rooms, remove dead cables and bring documentation up to date.

New Sites & Office Moves

Cabling for new builds, fit-outs and relocations, planned with your contractor’s schedule so the network is ready on moving day.

Good to know

Installed to recognized standards

Our installations follow international cabling standards including ANSI/TIA-568 and ISO/IEC 11801 and use components from established manufacturers. Where a system qualifies, we arrange the manufacturer’s system warranty for you.

FAQ

Common questions

Should we choose Cat6 or Cat6A?

Cat6 suits most office desks. Cat6A is worth the extra cost for Wi-Fi access points, links between cabinets and anywhere you may want 10-gigabit speeds later. We recommend point by point so you never pay for more than you need.

When do we need fiber?

Where distances exceed about 90 meters, between buildings, and for high-capacity backbone links.

Can you work in an occupied office?

Yes. We work evenings or weekends when needed, protect furniture and floors, and leave the site clean every day.

Do you provide test reports?

Always. A certified test result for every link is part of our standard handover.

Planning a new office or an upgrade?

Send us your floor plan and the number of points you need. We will come back with a design and a clear quotation.
