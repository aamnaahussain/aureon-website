CCTV & Security Systems

See clearly. Record reliably. Find footage in seconds.

A camera system is judged on the day you need it. The footage must exist, the face must be clear, the time stamp must be right, and someone has to find the clip quickly.

We design surveillance and access control around those moments: the right camera in the right position, properly recorded and easy to use.

What we deliver

Security system services

Site Survey & System Design

We walk the site with you, agree what each camera must see, and produce a layout drawing showing positions, fields of view, cable routes and storage calculations.

IP Camera Systems

High-resolution indoor and outdoor cameras chosen for each location: fixed, dome, PTZ, low-light and weatherproof models suited to the Saudi climate.

Recording & Video Management

Network video recorders and management software sized for your retention period, with redundancy for critical sites.

Video Analytics

Number plate recognition, people counting, line crossing and intrusion alerts, so staff are notified of events instead of watching screens all day.

Access Control & Time Attendance

Card, fingerprint and face recognition for doors and gates, linked to time and attendance, visitor management and your HR system.

Intercom & Gate Systems

Video intercoms, barriers and gate automation for offices, compounds and facilities.

Control Rooms & Remote Viewing

Video walls and operator workstations for larger sites, and secure mobile viewing for managers.

Maintenance Contracts

Scheduled health checks, cleaning, firmware updates and fast replacement of faulty units.

Good to know

Installed to meet local requirements

We design each system to meet the Kingdom’s regulations on security surveillance cameras and the requirements of the relevant authorities for your type of business, including camera placement, image quality and recording retention. For industrial facilities we also take the applicable industrial security directives into account.

Because cameras are network devices, we secure them too: default passwords changed, firmware updated, and the camera network separated from your business network.

FAQ

Common questions

How many cameras do we need?

It depends on your layout and what you need to see. We decide on site with you. A good design often uses fewer, better-placed cameras than expected.

How long will footage be kept?

We size storage to the retention period you need and that regulations require for your activity. The calculation is shown in the proposal.

Can we view cameras on our phones?

Yes. We set up secure remote viewing with individual user accounts and permissions.

Can you upgrade our old analog system?

Often, yes. In many cases we reuse existing cable routes and move you to IP in phases.

Is your site properly covered?

Book a free site survey. We will show you where the blind spots are and what it takes to close them.
