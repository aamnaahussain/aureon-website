FAQ

Common questions

How long does a website take?

A corporate website usually takes six to ten weeks from agreed scope to launch. Online stores and custom applications take longer. You will have a timeline before we begin.

Do we need a mobile app, or is a website enough?

If customers will use it often, need notifications, or need device features like the camera or location, an app makes sense. For many businesses a well-built mobile website is the right first step. We will advise honestly.

Can you take over an existing website or app?

Yes. We begin with a technical review and tell you whether to improve what exists or rebuild.

Who writes the content?

You know your business best. We can work from your material, or write and translate it for you, structured for readers and search engines.

Have an idea for a website or app?

Share it with us. We will respond with questions, suggestions and a realistic estimate.
