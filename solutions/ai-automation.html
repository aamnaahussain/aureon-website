What we deliver

AI & automation services

Workflow & Process Automation

Approvals, notifications, data entry and document routing on Microsoft Power Automate, Power Apps and similar platforms. Typical projects: purchase requests, leave and HR requests, onboarding, expense claims and IT tickets.

AI Assistants & Chatbots

Assistants that answer from your own policies, product information and knowledge base, in Arabic and English. They serve customers on your website and WhatsApp, or your staff inside Microsoft Teams.

Intelligent Document Processing

Invoices, delivery notes, contracts, IDs and forms are read automatically, Arabic included, checked, and posted to your ERP or finance system. Your team reviews only the exceptions.

Analytics & Forecasting

Your data sources connected into Power BI dashboards management can rely on, with forecasting for sales, demand, cash flow or maintenance where there is enough history.

ERP & Business System Integration

We connect to Microsoft Dynamics 365 and other ERP, CRM and HR platforms so information moves between systems without retyping.

AI Readiness & Governance

Where AI is appropriate, what data it may use, who is accountable. We write practical staff usage policies and check your approach against the PDPL and SDAIA’s AI ethics principles.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need a large budget to start with AI?

No. Many organizations already hold Microsoft 365 licenses that include automation tools. A first project is usually one process delivered in a few weeks, and its savings help fund the next.

Will our data be safe?

Every solution is designed so your data stays inside your own tenant or an approved environment. Before any AI tool touches personal or confidential information, we agree what it may access and where it is processed.

Can your solutions handle Arabic?

Yes. Assistants and document processing work in Arabic and English, including mixed-language documents. We test with your real documents before go-live.

Will automation replace our staff?

In our experience it removes the dullest part of the job. Teams usually redirect the time to work that was being neglected: follow-up, analysis and customer service.

Which task would you automate first?

Tell us about the process that wastes the most time. We will say honestly whether it can be automated and what it would take.
