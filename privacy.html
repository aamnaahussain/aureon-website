Privacy Policy

Who we are

Aureon Ventures & Technologies ("Aureon", "we") operates this website. This policy explains what personal data we collect through it, why, and how you can exercise your rights under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

What we collect

Details you send us through the contact form (such as name, company, email, phone number, city and your message), and basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, if analytics cookies are accepted.

Why we use it

To respond to your enquiry, to keep the website secure and working, and to understand how it is used so we can improve it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing us at the address on our Contact page.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website and email, under appropriate safeguards, or where the law requires it.

Questions? Email hussnain@aureon.sa.
